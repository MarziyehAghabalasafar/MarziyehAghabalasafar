## About Me

I am an MSc student in Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence), interested in Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Explainable AI. I enjoy developing data-driven solutions to real-world problems while building models that are not only accurate but also transparent and interpretable.

My experience comes from academic research and personal projects, where I have implemented machine learning models and data analysis workflows using Python, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, and Explainable AI libraries. Through these projects, I have worked on data preprocessing, feature engineering, feature selection, predictive modeling, model evaluation, and explainability techniques to better understand model behavior and support reliable decision-making.

I enjoy connecting with researchers, engineers, and professionals who are passionate about AI, Machine Learning, and data-driven innovation. Feel free to connect if you'd like to discuss ideas, research, or potential collaborations.
