## About

I am an MSc student in Computer Science specializing in Artificial Intelligence, with a research focus on machine learning, deep learning, network analysis, graph machine learning, and explainable AI (XAI). I am interested in developing reliable, transparent, and meaningful data-driven solutions for real-world research and decision-making.

Through academic research and project work, I have gained experience in data preprocessing, feature engineering, feature selection, predictive modeling, model evaluation, network analysis, and model interpretation. I work primarily with Python and apply methods and tools across machine learning, deep learning, graph learning, statistical analysis, visualization, and responsible AI.

I welcome connections with researchers, professors, and practitioners interested in artificial intelligence and data science.
