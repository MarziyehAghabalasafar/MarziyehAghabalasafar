## About

I am an MSc student in Computer Science with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence. My academic interests include Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Network Analysis, Graph Machine Learning, and Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI).

I develop data-driven solutions that combine predictive performance with transparency and interpretability. My work spans data preprocessing, feature engineering, feature selection, model development, evaluation, and explainability techniques for applications in research and real-world decision-making.
